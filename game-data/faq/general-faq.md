# Generic — Player FAQ

**Q: How do I transfer my save between PC and console?**
A: Cross-progression is enabled by default once you link your account under Settings > Account
> Linked Platforms. Progress syncs automatically when you quit to the main menu.

**Q: I bought the Deluxe Edition — where are my bonus items?**
A: Deluxe bonus items (the Warden cosmetic set and the soundtrack) are granted automatically on
first login and appear in your inventory under Cosmetics > Deluxe. If they're missing, verify
your purchase under Settings > Account > Purchase History before contacting support — this
resolves the large majority of "missing Deluxe items" reports without agent involvement.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Use the in-game Feedback option (default keybind F9) or contact support directly. Include
your platform, approximate time of the issue, and what you were doing when it happened.

**Q: Is there a way to disable controller vibration?**
A: Yes — Settings > Accessibility > Vibration. As of patch 1.4.0 this is independent from
overall rumble intensity.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refund requests are handled per platform storefront policy (Steam, PlayStation Store, Xbox
Store, etc.) — support can escalate but cannot directly process a refund.
